DistAlarm

An iPhone alarm that rings as you get close to your stop, so you can sleep on the bus or train.

The alarm did not ring

  • Set location to Always: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › DistAlarm › Always. With “While Using”, DistAlarm can only watch your stop while the app is open.
  • Allow Alarms for DistAlarm in Settings, so the alarm can ring through silent mode.
  • iOS can notice your arrival up to a minute late. If you ride fast, choose a bigger alarm zone.
  • GPS is weak underground (for example on the MRT), so arrivals there can be late or missed.

I switched a stop on while I was already there, and nothing rang

That is on purpose. DistAlarm only rings when you enter the alarm zone, so switching on Home while you are at home does not wake you.

How can I hear the alarm without travelling?

Open Settings in DistAlarm and tap Test alarm. It rings after about one second.

Other things to know

  • Up to 20 stops can be switched on at once (an iOS limit).
  • To delete a stop, swipe it left in the Stops tab, or open it and tap Delete Stop.
  • DistAlarm needs an iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026.

DistAlarm does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your location or anything else you enter in the app.

Location

DistAlarm uses your location on your iPhone to notice when you reach a stop you switched on, and to show how far away it is. iOS does this check on the phone itself. Your location is not sent to the developer or stored by DistAlarm.

What stays on your iPhone

Your saved stops, your settings, your ride names and speeds, and any alarm sounds you import are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Maps and search (Apple)

The map and place search are provided by Apple Maps. When you search, your search text and the visible map area are sent to Apple to find places. Apple handles this under the Apple Privacy Policy, and it is not shared with the developer.

Alarms and notifications

Alarms and notifications are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. Nothing is sent over the internet to deliver them.

Children

DistAlarm collects no data from anyone, including children.
